GlowNote Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

GlowNote is a platform that allows users to send and receive positive anonymous messages.

Information We Collect

  • Name provided during account creation
  • Username
  • Anonymous messages sent and received through the app
  • Device and usage information provided by Apple and Firebase services

How We Use Information

  • Provide and maintain the GlowNote service
  • Deliver anonymous messages to users
  • Improve app performance and reliability
  • Detect abuse and inappropriate content
