About GlowNote

GlowNote lets users send and receive kind, anonymous messages. The app does not support public posting, replies, chat, or direct messaging.

Safety

If you receive inappropriate content, please use the report option in the app or contact support.

Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your GlowNote account and associated profile information by contacting support.
